        5. Test before you assign. Run your prompt through an approved AI tool and see what it produces. If the output would earn a good grade, revisit the plan or add a checkpoint.

                    How to interpret the research base

                    Discipline examples are drawn from published course implementations, practice cases, and benchmark studies in which AI tools were run against real assessments. Evidence quality varies: most sources establish feasibility, disciplinary relevance, or the scale of the substitution risk, but do not establish scoring reliability across instructors or institutions. Pilot locally, align the rubric to the intended learning, and check scorer agreement before high-stakes use.

                    The five VALID-AI roles of AI and the six decision criteria come from the IU VALID-AI framework; the simulation design principles are synthesized from the simulation-based learning literature listed with each principle.

                    AIAS descriptions follow the current AI Assessment Scale 2.1. AIAS is a design aid, not evidence that a particular assessment is valid or reliably scored.
